## Interpretation of the close calls

`sp_014` is the closest analysis/hot-take boundary. The stated 3% change is explicitly used as a causal premise. Choosing analysis does not endorse the conclusion that shoes explain everything; Rule 1 does not require a sound or conclusive causal design. A hot-take reading remains defensible if the statistic is judged ornamental to the sweeping conclusion.

`sp_007` and `sp_010` make arguments from comparisons, so personal experience and weak experimental control do not remove their evidence. Conversely, `sp_021`, `sp_027`, and `sp_029` include specific details without developing an argument from them. Rule 3 requires both the fact and its argumentative role.
