## Three observed candidate distinctions

1. Some comments supply a link, hardware specification, measured result, or concrete
   experience. Others assert a conclusion without this support. Compare HN 41050420
   and HN 48087215 with HN 46990941.
2. Some comments ask a direct clarification question. Other question-shaped comments
   argue a position or give their own answer. Compare HN 47340345 with HN 44163185.
3. Some comments express immediate agreement, a joke, or excitement. Others give a
   substantive explanation. Compare HN 48292641 with HN 41054041.

The draft combines reactions with unsupported statements because the exact boundary
between "hot take" and "reaction" is weak in many short HN comments. This is a
proposal to discuss, not a student decision already recorded.

## Collection scope

Eight AI threads, sampled round-robin through each thread's comment tree. Included
comments have 5–180 whitespace-separated words. Comments are selected whole, never
cut to satisfy this range. Deleted/empty content and normalized duplicate text are
excluded. The range favors short discussions and underrepresents long arguments.
Nested replies can depend on missing conversational context and closely related
replies can cross the notebook's random split. Duplicate removal does not eliminate
that thread-level dependence. Report this limitation in Unit 6.

Collection selection is not based on labels. Label balance remains unknown until
the student completes annotation. Read collection/reading-pack.md for the 40 texts.
