# Check your own five criteria before committing

The assignment explicitly says the student writes all five criteria. This
checklist explains what a reviewer needs; it does not supply criteria or targets.

For each numbered criterion, write:

- Your numeric target and direction, such as a minimum or maximum.
- The measurement: metric, formula and dataset/subset being measured.
- Your pass/fail outcome. For training-based measures in Unit 6, clarify whether
  it must hold across all three seeds, as the course requires.
- A rule for a missing or empty subset if your metric could encounter one.
- Your reason for that number. At least three of the five reasons should name
  something specific about Hacker News, your labels, or actual counts. Do not
  invent a smallest-label count before annotation is complete.

Cover at least three of accuracy, per-label performance, balance, consistency,
and confidence. A vague word such as "good" or "reliable" without an operational
measurement cannot be checked. A number alone does not establish testability.

The assistant can check your wording and present one reason a target might be
too easy and one it might be too hard. The choices and numbers stay yours.
