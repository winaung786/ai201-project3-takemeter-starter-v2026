Label definitions and decision rules
  • analysis: Supports its main claim with a specific, checkable detail, concrete first-hand observation or relevant cited source.
  • unsupported: Primarily a statement, judgment, joke, agreement or reaction without specific support for its main point.
  • question: Primarily requests information or clarification without advancing its own answer or advocating a conclusion.

Supporting details must relate to the main claim. “I agree 100%” is not evidence. A quotation that the writer rejects does not by itself support the rejection. Rhetorical or self-answered questions follow the analysis/unsupported rule. Classify support present in the text; this is not fact-checking.
