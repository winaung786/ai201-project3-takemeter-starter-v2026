Proposed definitions
  • analysis: Supports its main claim with a specific, checkable detail, a concrete first-hand observation, or a cited source.
  • unsupported: Primarily a statement, judgment, joke, agreement, or reaction without specific support for its main point.
  • question: Primarily requests information or clarification without advancing its own answer or advocating a conclusion.

Specific support must relate to the main claim. "I agree 100%" is not evidence. A rhetorical or self-answered question follows the statement rule. Classifying support does not fact-check it.
